# Sales Performance Analysis and Interactive Dashboard

An interactive sales dashboard built in Advanced Excel to track revenue performance, identify trends, and support business decisions.

## Objective
Analyze one year of sales data to find which products, regions, channels, and customer groups drive revenue, and present the results in an interactive dashboard.

## Tools Used
- Advanced Excel: PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers, formulas

## Dataset
- 2,000 sales orders, January to December 2026 (sample dataset)
- Fields: Order ID, Date, Region, Sales Channel, Customer Type, Product Category, Product, Salesperson, Quantity, Unit Price, Discount, Revenue
- Coverage: 5 regions, 4 sales channels, 6 product categories, 15 salespeople

## Key Insights
- **Computers drive the business:** about 59% of revenue (20.6 lakh) from only 17% of orders. Gaming Laptop is the top product at 6.66 lakh.
- **Seasonality:** Q4 is the strongest quarter (28% of revenue) and Q3 the weakest (21.9%). June is the best month (3.74 lakh) and February the weakest (2.19 lakh).
- **Regions:** Central leads with 8.03 lakh (23%) and North follows closely at 7.87 lakh. West is the lowest at 5.87 lakh (17%).
- **Channels:** Online is the top channel (32%), followed by Corporate Sales (29%) and Retail Store (26%). Marketplace is the smallest at 12%.
- **Customers:** Returning customers generate about 68% of revenue and spend more per order (1,807 vs 1,588 for new customers).
- **Discounts:** About 86% of orders had a discount, worth roughly 2.8 lakh in total.
- **Salespeople:** Top performer is Mei Tan (3.2 lakh).

## Recommendations
- Protect and expand the Computers range, since it carries most of the revenue.
- Plan stock and promotions around the Q4 peak and the weaker February and Q3 periods.
- Focus growth efforts on West and South, the two lowest regions.
- Review how often discounts are given, as they apply to most orders.
- Strengthen retention, since returning customers are the most valuable group.
